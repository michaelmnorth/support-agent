# Response formats

The time lines always come from the tool (`slot_labels`). Everything else may flex to match the thread.

## Proposing times, email

```
Hi Jane,

Happy to get this on the calendar. Here are a few times that work on my end for the kickoff (30 min).
Times are in PT, with ET in parentheses:

• Mon, Nov 2 · 8:30–9:00 AM PT (11:30 AM–12:00 PM ET)
• Tue, Nov 3 · 8:30–9:00 AM PT (11:30 AM–12:00 PM ET)
• Wed, Nov 4 · 10:00–10:30 AM PT (1:00–1:30 PM ET)
• Thu, Nov 5 · 10:00–10:30 AM PT (1:00–1:30 PM ET)

If none of those work, send over a couple of windows that do and I will make it fit. Once you pick one
I will send a Teams invite.

Thanks,
Michael
```

## Proposing times, Slack or Teams chat

```
Hey Oliver! A few times that work for a call (45 min), in UK time (ET in parentheses):
• Tue, Nov 3 · 3:00–3:45 PM UK time (10:00–10:45 AM ET)
• Wed, Nov 4 · 2:00–2:45 PM UK time (9:00–9:45 AM ET)
Let me know which works and I will send the Teams invite.
```

## Confirmation

```
Hi Jane,

Perfect. We are set for Tue, Nov 3 · 11:00–11:30 AM PT (2:00–2:30 PM ET). A Teams invite is on its way.

Talk soon,
Michael
```

## Counter-proposal when their time fails `check_time`

```
Hi Jane,

Thursday at 3 runs into something on my end. Closest I can do:

• Thu, Nov 5 · 10:00–10:30 AM PT (1:00–1:30 PM ET)
• Fri, Nov 6 · 9:00–9:30 AM PT (12:00–12:30 PM ET)

Either work?

Thanks,
Michael
```

## The ping to the owner (ask_then_send)

```
Scheduling request from Jane Doe (email) for kickoff, 30 min.
Proposed (client zone PT / yours ET):
• Mon, Nov 2 · 8:30–9:00 AM PT (11:30 AM–12:00 PM ET)
• Tue, Nov 3 · 8:30–9:00 AM PT (11:30 AM–12:00 PM ET)
Draft saved in the thread. Reply "send" to send it, "edit" to change it, or "skip".
```

## Style rules

- First names. No "hope this finds you well". No exclamation marks in email; one is fine in Slack.
- Offer 2 to 4 options, never more. Different days before same-day pairs.
- Always say which zone leads and which is in parentheses.
- One ask per message. End with what happens next ("I will send a Teams invite").
